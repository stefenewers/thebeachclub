# Frankfort / Prospect venue reference

Purpose: anchor BEACH CLUB concept media to a real Jamaican North Coast location without representing Prospect Estate as a confirmed venue.

## Venue facts to preserve in concept work

- Prospect Estate sits on Jamaica's north coast near Ocho Rios, between the Caribbean Sea and lush mountain greenery.
- Frankfort is a private compound with two buildings, a modern pool, gazebo, tropical lawn and a private beach.
- Prospect describes Frankfort's beach as approximately 300 feet of powdery white sand.
- Published wedding/event guidance lists Frankfort Villa and/or Private Beach at 75 seated guests. This does not establish standing-event capacity and must not be used as proof that a 500-person BEACH CLUB event is approved or feasible.

## BEACH CLUB transformation rules

The concept should look like an exceptional existing Jamaican private beach transformed for one day, not a permanent European superclub.

Preserve:
- existing shoreline proportions
- dense North Coast vegetation
- white/pale natural sand
- turquoise Caribbean water
- existing villa/pool/gazebo relationships where visible
- intimate scale and strong natural shade

Add selectively:
- 8–15 yellow/orange umbrellas in loose clusters
- low natural timber/white-linen lounge setups
- 3–6 premium cabana/lounge zones
- one compact champagne bar
- one compact premium spirits bar
- one low DJ booth
- discreet event signage
- 150–250 visible patrons in the hero frame, with the overall event capable of implying a larger 500-person attendance across the property

Avoid:
- large festival stage
- LED walls
- truss forests
- symmetrical resort umbrella grids
- multi-storey temporary structures
- giant pools or constructed decks that do not exist
- oversized sponsor sculptures
- unrealistic yacht flotillas
- generic Mediterranean cliffs or architecture

## Status

Prospect Estate is a visual/concept reference only. Venue is not confirmed. All sponsor-facing usage should describe this as a conceptual North Coast private-estate environment until written venue approval is secured.
